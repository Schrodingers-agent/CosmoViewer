lod
---
u = splitmix64(id ^ seed) top 24 bits.  same particle same u every snapshot  -> no flicker in movies
(row-based u: 11% overlap between frames. id-hash: 99%.)
24 bits ~ 48 usable levels. MAX_LEVEL 80 is decorative.

store: u < cap/N, cap 16M, sorted by leaf cell (128^3) then u
  first k of a cell = uniform sample of the cell
  levels nested -> zoom never pops
  >= 8M particles -> mmap cache, 8GB LRU, ~/.cosmoviewer/lod_cache

f(d) = min(1, f_focus * (D/max(d,D))^2)
  flat to the focus, 1/d^2 behind. without the falloff a zoom never gets sharp
  f_focus = finest 2^(-k/2) that fits X
  leftover budget at full res -> widen, store only
cells needing f > store fraction -> read from files, only blocks touching the frustum

X = 2M. gadgetviewer default + >1 particle/pixel at 1600x1000.

eagle 850M, mac: 1.46-1.99M drawn all the way down, full res at the focus by 0.004 box.
mem: 3.5-4GB peak zooming, 1.7-2.2 idle (remeasured. the 0.9 idle was optimistic).
new set lands: 600ms in the container, <140ms native. colouring-by-property not timed natively.

nothing here is ever measured. float32 on purpose.
